        How to use

        From loading a model to watching the command go out.

        1. Load your model — paste your Teachable Machine URL and press Load model.
        2. Start the camera — allow camera access when the browser asks.
        3. Connect micro:bit — pair your board over Bluetooth.
        4. Show a gesture — hold one of the mapped gestures in front of the camera.
        5. Watch the command — above 80% confidence it is sent to the robot.
        6. Or drive it by hand — switch to Manual test and press-and-hold a direction; the robot stops the moment you let go.
